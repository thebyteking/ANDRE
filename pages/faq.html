FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I AM TRAVELLING TO NETHERLANDS IN JULY. WE WILL BE LANDING IN AMSTERDAM AND ATTENDING A CONCERT IN MAASTRECHT. I AM CONSIDERING BUYING AN FLIGHT-HOTEL-CAR PACKAGE IN AMSTERDAM AND DRIVING TO MAASTRECHT THEN DRIVING BACK TO AMSTERDAM FOR THE FLIGHT BACK HOME RATHER THAN BOOKING ANOTHER FLIGHT FROM AMSTERDAM TO MAASTRECHT AND BACK. IS THIS A GOOD IDEA?

For most visitors, taking the train is easier than renting a car just for Amsterdam–Maastricht. Direct trains connect the cities, with a typical journey of about 2½ hours each way. Driving is roughly 220 km and takes around 2½ hours in ideal conditions; allow closer to 3 hours for traffic. The train also avoids parking and city-driving hassles. A car could still make sense if you’re a group, have a lot of luggage, plan to visit places beyond the cities, or the package makes the car very inexpensive. Compare the full extra cost—rental, fuel, parking, insurance, and any drop-off fees—against train tickets for everyone. If the concert ends late, check the return train schedule for that specific date; staying overnight in Maastricht may be more comfortable than rushing back. I’d generally book the flight and Amsterdam hotel package separately from the Maastricht transport, then take the train. If you do rent a car, consider picking it up only for the Maastricht portion rather than keeping it in Amsterdam.

What are the costs to travel from Amsterdam to Maastricht 


For Amsterdam to Maastricht, budget roughly:

Option	One way, per person	Return
Train (2nd class)	From €33.30	From €66.60
Bus	About €18–€28	About €36–€56

The train takes about 2½–2¾ hours; the direct bus takes around 3 hours. Fares vary by date and availability,
so these are estimates, not guaranteed prices.

For a car, the distance is about 210–220 km each way. The total depends heavily on rental price, fuel, parking,
and whether you keep the car for your Amsterdam stay; check the package’s rental terms and fuel policy before
comparing. For a solo traveler or couple, train or bus will often be cheaper than renting a car solely for this trip.

If by “July” you mean July 2027, exact fares may not be available yet. Check the operator’s booking
planner once your travel date is set.

What would a taxi cost?

A taxi from Amsterdam to Maastricht will likely cost around €400–€500 one way on the meter. A pre-booked private transfer may start around €300 one way, depending on the vehicle and date. The drive is about 217 km and takes a little over 2 hours without heavy traffic. city-airport-taxis.com So a taxi round trip could be roughly €800–€1,000, before any waiting time or other charges. Get a fixed-price quote for your exact pickup and drop-off points before booking; for comparison, the train is far cheaper.
