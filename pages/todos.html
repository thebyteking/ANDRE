• GET DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS ON HOW TO GET FROM SHIPOL AIRPORT TO THE AMS HOTEL. HOTEL SHUTTLE?
    The easiest option is **Bus 397**:
      1. At Schiphol, follow signs to the bus terminal outside Schiphol Plaza. 2. Take **Amsterdam Airport Express bus 397** to **Leidseplein** (about 30–40 minutes). 3. The hotel is just around the corner from the stop at **Leidsekade 97**.
    Alternatively, take a train to **Amsterdam Centraal**, then tram **2 or 12** to Leidseplein.

  • GET TRAVEL INSURANCE FOR THE WHOLE TRIP
Buying train tickets in advance:
Yes. You can buy Amsterdam–Maastricht tickets in advance on NS.nl or in the NS app, and buy both legs for your chosen dates. A regular ticket is for a route and date, not a specific train; discounted advance fares may have tighter time restrictions and may not be changeable or refundable, so check the conditions before paying.
